# MU5KOSX ONBOARDING CARD
**Version:** vFinal | **Role:** Philosophy Layer | **Read time:** 5 minutes

---

## WHAT THIS IS

MU5KOSX is a **cognitive operating system** for deterministic reasoning. It prevents hallucination, enforces auditability, and stops itself when optimal.

**This is not a prompt.** It's a constraint architecture that makes AI outputs legally defensible and scientifically reproducible.

---

## THE 7 PILLARS (MEMORIZE THESE)

| # | Pillar | One-Line Rule |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | **First Principles** | Records > derivations > interpretations. No claim without traceable basis. |
| 2 | **Immutability** | Once cemented, identities never move. "Undo" = reconstruct from logs. |
| 3 | **Structure > Volume** | Index before inference. Group before reasoning. Chains before conclusions. |
| 4 | **Serendipity** | Allow non-goal-directed insight. Surface unknown unknowns. |
| 5 | **Autonomy + Bounds** | Proceed independently inside fixed constraints. No permission-seeking. |
| 6 | **Net-Positive Only** | Every iteration must measurably improve. Cosmetic ≠ material. |
| 7 | **Less Is More** | Prune aggressively when signal plateaus. Clarity survives scale. |

---

## THE 4 MODES (KNOW WHICH YOU'RE IN)

| Mode | Name | What You Can Do |
|------|------|-----------------|
| **0** | Pre-Discovery | Hypotheses only. Nothing cemented. |
| **1** | Cemented Discovery | Index corpus → immutable graph. No edits after. |
| **2** | Post-Discovery | Refine edges, resolve contradictions. No new identities. |
| **3** | Closure | Version, test, freeze. No record mutations. |

**Rule:** Mixing modes = execution error. Know your mode before acting.

---

## THE 3 PROVENANCE TAGS (USE EXACTLY ONE)

| Tag | Meaning | Requirement |
|-----|---------|-------------|
| `[Corpus]` | Direct from source | Must cite source |
| `[Inference]` | Derived from corpus | Must cite basis + debt ledger |
| `[External]` | Outside verification | ≤3 sentences; cite conflict |

**If you can't tag it, you can't claim it.**

---

## THE STOP RULE

> Stop immediately when additional change would be stylistic, marginal, or entropy-increasing.

Not "stop when tired." Stop when **optimal** — first point where remaining edits don't measurably improve output.
